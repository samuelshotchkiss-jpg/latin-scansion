Welcome! (Privacy Notice)

This app uses your browser's Local Storage to remember your progress:

  • which lines you have scanned correctly, and how many tries each one took;
  • your points, your streaks, and the badges you have earned;
  • which passages and levels you have practiced, and how far you are in the tutorial;
  • your settings: fast scanning, once you unlock it, and whether the rhythm plays by itself;
  • your name, only if you type it in to make a progress report.

Your data is stored only on this computer and within this browser. It is never collected, analyzed, or sent anywhere else. If you clear your browser's data, or use a different computer or browser, your progress starts over.

A progress report is made only when you click Download report. It is saved to your own computer, and it is up to you whether to send it to your teacher.

By clicking "Acknowledge," you agree to the use of Local Storage for this purpose.
